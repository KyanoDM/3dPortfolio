ABOUT

About Me

Hi, I'm Kjell, the 3D modeler behind 3D Visualizer. I focus on stylised and hard-surface props, working end-to-end in Blender: modeling, UVs, texturing and optimization. On every commission I work with a concept artist first, so the final model is original and matches the brief before I touch a single vertex.

I've been active in the 3D community for about 10 months, and I'm currently open to new freelance commissions.

PROCESS

How I Work

1 Modeling

  • Mostly stylised, with range from low-poly to high-poly
  • Hard-surface focus: props, weapons, objects
  • Full workflow in Blender, start to finish
  • Organic modeling (characters, food) not yet part of my specialty

2 Texturing

  • Hand-painted or fully procedural textures
  • Diffuse, roughness, bump and metallic maps
  • Built to be easy to adjust and refine later
  • Every project ships with a full map breakdown
